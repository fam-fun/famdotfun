# famdotfun

**Good things come to the fam.**

famdotfun is a launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Launch a coin, find your people, get paid together. Every trade gives back to the fam, every hour.

- **Launch a coin** in a few steps, paired with ETH or USDG.
- **Trade** on the curve until the coin graduates to its own Uniswap v4 pool.
- **Get paid**: the trading fees on every coin pay its creator, and Payday pays the traders of each hour.
